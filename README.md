## Overview

Vera is an evidence-based women's health platform designed to combat medical misinformation by transforming scientific research into clear, accessible, and trustworthy knowledge.

The platform focuses on transparency, evidence quality, and a premium editorial reading experience rather than opinion-based health content.

---

## Features

- Evidence-backed health topics
- Scientific verdict pages
- Editorial reading experience
- Intelligent topic discovery
- Responsive design
- Scroll-based immersive storytelling
